A second pair of eyes

When a message asks you to pay, tap or hurry, Stead helps you pause, and brings family in before you act.

The problem

Scams don’t rely on carelessness. They rely on perfect timing.

A parcel you’re expecting. A fine you’re worried about. A “new number” from your son. The message fits your day, so there’s no reason to stop and think. It happens in Seoul, London, Kuala Lumpur and New York alike.

They arrive when they make sense

Scammers send thousands of believable messages. Some land at exactly the wrong moment, when they match something real.

Parcel on holdUnpaid tollHi Mum, new numberAccount lockedGuaranteed returnsYou’ve won+ many more

Checkers only help if you already suspect

Most scam-checking tools wait for you to copy and paste a message you’re already unsure about. By then, the risky tap may have happened.

Message arrives→tap, pay, done

Family hears about it afterwards

The people who would gladly help check usually find out once the money has gone, not while there was still time to ask.

What Stead does

Understand. Explain. Connect. People verify.

Understand

AI reads what a message claims to be, what it asks you to do, and the pressure it uses: urgency, payment, unfamiliar links, secrecy.

Explain

A calm, plain-language warning with one safe next step, like checking the courier’s own app. No jargon, no fake percentages.

Connect

With your permission, a trusted person gets a short safety case, taps “I’m checking”, and you see the update live.

For families

Family help, not surveillance.

◎

Consent first

Nothing is shared until the protected person agrees. They choose who helps and what kinds of alerts are shared.

≡

Only what’s needed

Guardians see a short summary, the warning signs and a safe next step. Not a feed of private chats.

✓

AI advises, people decide

Stead never pays, blocks, opens links or contacts anyone. Policy in plain code decides when to alert family, not the AI alone.

⇄

Honest about uncertainty

If the AI fails or isn’t sure, Stead says “verify independently”. It never shows “safe” or an invented scam score.

The guardian gets a simple dashboard: which cases need a look, which are being checked, and which are resolved. Updates appear on both screens live, without refreshing.
